## Mini Travel Guide: My Favorite City - Rome

Hello! My name is **Maria**. I am a creative, spontaneous, and passionate person who loves art, history, and photography. I enjoy discovering ancient places and soaking in the culture of vibrant cities.

In this guide, I want to share my absolute favorite city: Rome.

---

## Points of Interest & My Top 3
If you visit Rome, here is my *Top 3* list of unforgettable historic sites to explore:

1. **The Colosseum**: An iconic symbol of ancient Roman history and engineering.
2. **Trevi Fountain**: A beautiful baroque masterpiece where you must toss a coin to return to Rome.
3. **The Pantheon**: One of the best-preserved ancient monuments with a stunning open dome.

---

## Local Flavors & What to Pack
As a traveler who loves enjoying life, here are my top recommendations for Rome:

* **Authentic Pasta Carbonara**: A classic, simple, and delicious Italian dish.
* **Gelato**: Trying fresh artisanal ice cream while strolling through historic streets.
* **A good camera**: Essential for capturing the artistic architecture around every corner.
* **An open mind**: Ready to embrace Italian culture and history.
